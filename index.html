About CoolFlow

Cool Flow is a premier multi-media and musical collective established in the vibrant heart of Cebu, Philippines. Formed in early 2023, the group was born from a shared vision among student-artists at Rosemont Hills Montessori College in Danao City. What started as a campus collaboration quickly flowed into the local scene, specializing in high-fidelity live performances, cinematic visual storytelling, and curated soundtracks for life's biggest milestones.
